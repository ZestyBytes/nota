![The dragon tree, thin canes topped with a fountain of narrow leaves](plant-dragon-tree.jpg)

Slow, patient and almost impossible to kill by neglect. Thin woody canes with a spray of narrow leaves edged in red, and a habit of leaning towards the light until you turn it.

## Care

- The most common mistake is too much water. Let the top half of the pot dry out before watering again, and much less in winter.
- Fussy about fluoride in tap water, which shows as brown tips and yellow edges. Rainwater or stood water suits it better.
- Lower leaves yellowing and dropping is normal ageing, not a problem. Pull them off cleanly.
- Shade is what it is getting, so expect it to stretch between leaves and to fade at the edges. Neither hurts it.
- Cut a cane back in spring if it gets too tall; it branches from just below the cut.
- It copes with shade, but grows leggier and loses the red in the leaf edge. Turn it each time you water or it will lean towards the door.
