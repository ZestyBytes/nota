Six months of keeping seven house plants alive in the office, with a photograph
of each one at the start of every month so the difference is visible rather
than remembered.

The seven: the asparagus fern, the devil's ivy, the dragon tree, the parlour
palm, the peace lily, the philodendron Birkin and the spider plant.

Watering is already handled. Each one carries its own cadence and its last
watering date, and the rota on the House plants page works out who is thirsty
and puts them in order. This journey is not the rota, it is the record of
whether the rota is actually working.

What each month's check-in covers: a photograph of every plant, anything that
has visibly changed, whether the light where it stands is enough as the autumn
comes in, and anything repotted, fed or lost.

Month one is the baseline. The photographs matter more than the words here,
because in March the only useful question is what these looked like in
September.
