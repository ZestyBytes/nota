Anything written here shows as the task's note, and the task becomes tappable.
Leave it empty for a plain one-line to-do.

Mark it done either way: add `done: true` to the frontmatter, which is the
quick one on a phone, or put an exact date in `completedAt`, for example
`completedAt: "YYYY-MM-DD"`. With `done: true` the day you saved the file is
taken as the day it was finished.

Completed tasks move to "Recently completed" at the foot of the To-do page,
struck through, with the five most recent kept.
