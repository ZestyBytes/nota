![Banana bread cut into wedges on a board, with a buttered slice alongside](attachments/banana-bread.jpg)

The one that gets made when the fruit bowl has gone too far. Five bananas is a
lot, and that is the point: it bakes dense and damp rather than cakey, and it
is better on the second day than the first.

## You'll need

- 150g baking spread, plus a little for the tin
- 340g plain flour
- 210g caster sugar
- 5 bananas, the browner the better
- 2 eggs
- 75ml milk
- 120g walnuts, chopped
- A handful of sultanas

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C and grease a loaf tin with baking spread so nothing
   sticks.
2. Beat the baking spread and sugar together until it lightens and loosens.
3. Beat in the eggs one at a time.
4. Mash the bananas and stir them through, then the milk.
5. Fold in the flour, then the walnuts and sultanas. Stop as soon as it comes
   together; the less it is worked, the better the crumb.
6. Tip into the tin, level the top and bake for 1 hour. A skewer into the
   middle should come out clean.
7. Leave it in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a rack. Slice it
   warm and put butter on it.
