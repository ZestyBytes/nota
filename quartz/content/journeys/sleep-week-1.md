Around four hours a night for the past five days, off the back of being unwell.
That is half of what I need and I can feel every missing hour of it.

Eight hours is the target. Not an average I get to over a good fortnight, a
number I am actually trying to hit most nights.

Check-in is weekly: the average for the seven nights, and honestly how I felt
on it. The average alone lies, because four good nights and three terrible ones
average out to something that looks fine and did not feel fine at all.

## The scale

Weekly check-ins carry one of these alongside the number.

- Rough. Running on nothing, short with people, no useful thinking done.
- Flat. Functioning, but everything costs more than it should.
- Fine. Neither helped nor hindered by how I slept.
- Good. Woke up before the alarm and did not resent the day.
- Excellent. The kind of week where everything else in Noted gets easier.

This week: rough.
