Zero hours. I cannot play a note and I cannot read music, so this is a genuine
start rather than a restart.

The week is split two ways. Study is the theory: reading the notation, learning
what the marks on the page are asking for, so that eventually the page is
instruction rather than decoration. Practice is the hands: scales, exercises,
and the actual physical business of finding the keys without looking.

Two hours a week to begin with. One studying, one practising, five days out of
seven at fifteen or twenty minutes rather than one heroic Sunday session.

Songs are the reason any of this is worth doing, so I want a piece on the go at
all times, even a badly played one. Enjoying it is not a distraction from
learning it.

The bar counts cumulative hours towards a hundred. Check in weekly with the
hours logged and whatever is currently being butchered.
