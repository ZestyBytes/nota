The rule is deliberately small: fifteen minutes a day with my heart rate
actually up. Not thirty, not an hour. Fifteen, because a target I can hit on a
bad day is the only kind that survives a bad day.

Three things count.

A HIIT session at home is the quickest way to clear it, and the one to reach
for when the day has run away.

Ten thousand steps clears it comfortably, and mostly it will be Martha doing
the work. Two walks a day with the dog is close to the whole target on its own.

Anything else that gets me properly out of breath counts too, and I am not
going to be precious about which.

The bar counts days kept, ninety of them. Starting at zero today.
