Ten minutes from home, and a family favourite: milkshakes about as fresh as
they come, out of a vending machine in a shed.

The milk is from [Woodhorn Farm](https://www.woodhornfarm.co.uk/) just up the
road, so it has not travelled far to get here. You pick your flavour and your
size, the machine does the rest, and that is the whole transaction. I had
strawberry. Ruby had chocolate.

You can buy a reusable litre bottle if you are stocking up, or take a free
500ml cup with a lid and a straw, which is what we do. Bench outside, in the
shade.
