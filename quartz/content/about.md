<p class="page-deck">A personal record built from simple files, designed to stay useful for a lifetime.</p>

Nota is a personal journal, life archive and library built from ordinary Markdown files.

Writing happens privately in Obsidian. This public website is generated by Quartz and hosted as static files. It has no public editor, upload form, database or write API.

Only notes with the property `publish: true` are included. Private notes, templates and Obsidian settings are ignored by the public build.

## The model

Content type, topic and date are independent:

- Types include Journal, Note, Reading, Quote, Journey, Task, Event and Collection.
- Topics include Technology, ADHD, Gardening, Family, Music and anything added later.
- Dates can include `createdAt`, `occurredAt`, `publishedAt`, `dueAt` and `completedAt`.

The files remain readable and portable even without Obsidian or this website.
