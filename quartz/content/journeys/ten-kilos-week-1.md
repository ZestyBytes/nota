Standing on the scales properly for the first time in about two years. 92kg,
which is roughly what I expected and still not what I wanted to see.

The plan is deliberately dull: walk every day, cook at home four nights out of
seven, and weigh in once a week on a Monday morning before breakfast so the
number means the same thing each time. No app, no points, no photographs of
food. Just a line in Noted every Monday.

Ten kilos is the goal because 82kg is where I felt best, and because a round
number is easier to stay honest about than a vague intention to be healthier.
