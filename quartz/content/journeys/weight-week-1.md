108kg. The heaviest I've been. I don't much like writing it down, but that's
where I'm starting.

90kg is the target. It is where I felt best and it is far enough away to be
worth the effort. 100kg is the milestone I am going to actually celebrate,
because eight kilos is a real change and waiting eighteen kilos for the first
bit of credit is how these things get abandoned.

Weigh-in is once a week, Friday morning, before breakfast, so the number means
the same thing every time. Everything else lives in the other journeys: the
moving is in Fifteen minutes a day, the sleep is in Eight hours.
