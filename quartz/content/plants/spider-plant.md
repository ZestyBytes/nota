![The spider plant arching out of a grey pot](plant-spider-plant.jpg)

The forgiving one. It will put up with a missed watering, a dark corner and a draught, and still throw out arching striped leaves. When it is happy it sends out long stems with baby plants on the end, which root in a glass of water in a fortnight.

## Care

- Water when the top of the compost dries out. It stores water in fat white roots, so it would rather be dry than soggy.
- Brown leaf tips usually mean tap water rather than neglect. Leave a jug to stand overnight, or use rainwater.
- Cut the pups off once they have stubby roots and pot them up, or leave them on for the fountain look.
- Repot when the roots start lifting the plant out of its pot, roughly every couple of years.
- Safe around cats and dogs, though a cat will happily chew it.
- In a dim corner the cream stripe fades back towards plain green, and it stops throwing pups. Near the door keeps both.
