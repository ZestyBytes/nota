![The Mini on the seafront, the evening I got it home](assets/journal/first-mini-seafront.jpg)

Found it on eBay on Thursday night, in bed, not really looking. Train up to
Barnsley on Friday morning, and drove it home.

I have always wanted a Mini, and always wanted one in orange, so getting
both at once still feels like a fluke rather than a purchase.

A thousand cc and under 50bhp, so it took the entire day: averaging about
45mph, with AA cover arranged from every angle I could think of. I had more
or less accepted I would be sleeping in it on the hard shoulder of a
motorway somewhere. It made it the whole way. I do not think I stopped
grinning.

The second photograph is my dad's first Mini, registered the same year as
mine: both K reg.
